Onboarding Guidelines

Mandatory standards every restaurant partner must agree to before listing on BankiBites, Banki, Cuttack, Odisha 754008.

Banki, Cuttack, Odisha 754008
Effective:
Version 1.0

This Onboarding Agreement is executed on the date affixed below at Banki, Cuttack, Odisha 754008, between BankiBites (operated under AkCreation, hereinafter referred to as "the Platform") and the undersigned Restaurant Partner (hereinafter referred to as "the Restaurant"), setting out the mandatory standards the Restaurant undertakes to observe while listed on the Platform.

Core Policies
Clause 1 — Packaging Standards

Premium Packaging is Mandatory

  1. All food must be packed in food-grade sealed containers or aluminium foil paper — no newspaper or open packets.
  2. Liquids and gravies must use leak-proof containers with sealed lids so food stays fresh and intact till delivery.
Clause 2 — Menu Availability

Notify BankiBites of Any Unavailable Items

  1. Inform BankiBites every morning about items you cannot prepare, and immediately for any item permanently removed from the menu.
Penalty: Failure to notify → 24-hour delisting. 3+ violations in 30 days → permanent removal.
Clause 3 — Food Quality & Customer Satisfaction

Restaurant Bears Full Responsibility

  1. All food quality, taste, hygiene, and safety is entirely the restaurant's responsibility. BankiBites is not liable.
  2. Refunds or replacements arising from food complaints are fully borne by the restaurant.
General Conduct
Clause 4 — Partner Code of Conduct

Platform Rules

  1. Be ready to accept orders during your listed operating hours at all times.
  2. Never collect direct payments from BankiBites customers outside the platform.
Clause 5 — Exit / Opt-Out

Governed by a Separate Opt-Out Declaration

  1. Exit from BankiBites — whether initiated by BankiBites (policy violation) or by the restaurant owner — is governed by a separate Opt-Out Declaration, signed at the time of removal.
  2. Re-joining later requires fresh acceptance of the then-current policy; the current version does not carry over.

Delivery Partner Policy

Delivery Partner
Onboarding Guidelines

Mandatory rules and requirements every delivery rider must agree to before joining BankiBites, Banki, Cuttack, Odisha 754008.

Banki, Cuttack, Odisha 754008
Effective:
Version 1.0

This Onboarding Agreement is executed on the date affixed below at Banki, Cuttack, Odisha 754008, between BankiBites (operated under AkCreation, hereinafter referred to as "the Platform") and the undersigned Delivery Partner (hereinafter referred to as "the Rider"), setting out the mandatory rules the Rider undertakes to observe while delivering for the Platform.

Core Policies
Clause 1 — Availability Notification Mandatory

Inform BankiBites by 10:00 AM if Unavailable

  1. Notify BankiBites before 10:00 AM every day you cannot work. No notification = you are considered on duty.
  2. Any delivery assigned after 10:00 AM without prior notice is your full responsibility to complete.
Penalty: Abandoning an assigned delivery without prior notice → 48-hour suspension (first offence). Repeated violations → permanent removal.
Clause 2 — Delivery Pricing Mandatory

Fixed Zone-Based Delivery Charges

ZoneCoverage AreaCharge
Zone A All locations within Banki excl. Bedapur & Gopalapur ₹ 30
Zone B Beyond Bedapur & Gopalapur ₹ 40
Outer Zone Locations outside the above zones ₹ 10 / km

For any location outside Zone A / Zone B, the delivery charge is ₹ 10 per kilometre and will be communicated by BankiBites before the order is accepted. All charges are inclusive — no extra collection from customers is permitted under any circumstance.

Clause 3 — Required Documents Mandatory

Aadhaar & Driving Licence Required

Aadhaar CardFront & back copy required
Driving LicenceValid copy for your vehicle
  1. Submit documents to the BankiBites coordinator before your first delivery. Onboarding will not proceed without them.
General Conduct
Clause 4 — Rider Code of Conduct

Platform Rules

  1. Handle all food packages with care — do not tilt, squeeze, or mishandle during transit.
  2. Maintain polite, professional behaviour with customers and restaurant staff at all times.
