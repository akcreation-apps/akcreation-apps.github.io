This Declaration of Formal Exit is executed on the date affixed below at Banki, Cuttack, Odisha 754008, between BankiBites (operated under AkCreation, hereinafter referred to as "the Platform") and the undersigned Restaurant Partner (hereinafter referred to as "the Restaurant"), acknowledging the formal opt-out of the Restaurant from the Platform on the terms recorded herein.

Reason for Opt-Out
Section 1 — Ground of Exit Tick One

Why is the Restaurant Being Opted Out

Removed by BankiBites Due to violation of the Restaurant Partner Policy — e.g. repeated unavailability without notice, food safety issues, direct off-platform collection, or misconduct.
Requested by the Restaurant Owner Voluntary exit as per the owner's written request.
Section 2 — Terms of Exit Mandatory

Effect of Opt-Out & Re-Joining

  1. The restaurant is formally opted out and delisted from BankiBites with effect from the date signed below — no further orders, listing, or platform support.
  2. Final settlement — BankiBites will pay the restaurant for all orders completed up to the exit date as per the normal end-of-day cycle. Any food quality complaints raised before the exit date remain the restaurant's responsibility to address.
  3. All BankiBites branding, banners, and QR codes must be removed from the premises, and the BankiBites name must not be used further.
  4. Re-joining later requires a fresh onboarding and acceptance of the then-current policy. Prior records, ratings, and menu data will not be restored.
This declaration is final. Once signed, the exit cannot be reversed by verbal request.

By signing below, the restaurant owner acknowledges the formal opt-out from BankiBites, confirms understanding of all terms above, and accepts that re-joining in future requires fresh acceptance of the then-current Restaurant Partner Policy.
