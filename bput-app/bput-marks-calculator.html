How to Use

  1. Enter Number of Subjects and your SGPA/CGPA.
  2. Optionally, enter Total Marks from syllabus (if unknown, we assume 100 per subject).
  3. Click Calculate Marks to see your percentage and secured marks.

📌 Important Notes

  • 📘 SGPA: Enter the SGPA for a single semester only. Also, provide the number of subjects for that semester.
  • 📊 CGPA: Enter the CGPA across one or more semesters. Provide the total number of subjects and total marks combined from all semesters included in the CGPA.
  • 💡 Example: If your CGPA is 8.5 for 2 semesters with 8 subjects each, total subjects = 16. Use total marks = sum of all semester marks.
