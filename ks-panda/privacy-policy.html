Privacy Policy

KS Panda — Employee Attendance App

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Overview

KS Panda is an employee attendance Android application developed by AKCreation Apps. It helps employees punch in and out of their assigned work sites, optionally with geofence verification, and gives administrators and managers tools to review attendance.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and your rights. We are committed to protecting your privacy and operate in compliance with Google Play's User Data Policy.

Information We Collect

KS Panda collects only the information required to record and verify your attendance:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a unique Google user ID, obtained when you sign in with Google. This is used to create and identify your employee profile.
  • Profile details — the full name and mobile number you provide during the initial signup step, along with your role (employee / manager / admin), approval status, and the work sites assigned to you by your administrator.
  • Attendance records — each punch (in or out) records the punch type, the selected site, the timestamp, and your device's location (latitude, longitude, GPS accuracy) at the moment of the punch.
  • Edit requests — if you request a correction to a punch, the requested time, type, and your reason are stored together with the original punch reference.
  • Device location — the app reads your current GPS location only when you tap Punch In or Punch Out. Location is required for geofence-enabled sites to verify you are physically on-site; for sites without a configured geofence, the location is best-effort and does not block the punch.
  • Local preferences — your theme choice (system / light / dark) and biometric lock preference are saved on your device only.

KS Panda does not collect your contact list, photos, SMS, call logs, microphone input, camera input, or browsing history.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and link each punch to your identity.
  • To verify you are within the allowed geofence of the selected work site at the time of punch.
  • To calculate daily, weekly, and monthly worked hours in your history view.
  • To notify you with a local reminder if you forget to punch out at the end of a shift.
  • To let managers and administrators review attendance for their teams.
  • To process edit-request approvals or rejections initiated by your manager.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any form of commercial sale. We do not share your data with any third party outside the systems described below.

Permissions Used

  • INTERNET — required to sign in and to read/write your profile, sites, and attendance records in our secure backend.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — required to capture your location when you punch in or out, so the geofence check can confirm you are at the correct site. Location is only read in response to your tap on the Punch button and when the Home screen is open (to show live distance from the selected site). The app does not track your location continuously in the background.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — required (on Android 13+) to show the local "forgot to punch out" reminder.
  • VIBRATE — used only to provide short haptic feedback when you tap the Punch button.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC (implicit) — used only when you opt in to the biometric app-lock. Biometric verification is performed entirely on-device by Android; no biometric data ever leaves your device or is sent to our servers.

Where Your Data Is Stored

KS Panda uses Google Firebase as its secure backend:

  • Firebase Authentication handles your Google sign-in.
  • Cloud Firestore stores your profile, assigned sites, attendance punches, and edit requests inside a dedicated namespace (attn_* collections) within the akcreation-apps Firebase project.
  • Access to these collections is restricted by Firebase security rules so that employees can only read and write their own records, and only approved managers and administrators can view team data.
  • All communication between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Firebase itself may process limited diagnostic information (for example, device model and crash signals) as described in Firebase's Privacy Information.

Administration Panel

Users whose profile is marked as admin or manager may open an administration dashboard from the Profile screen. This launches https://akcreation-apps.com/ks-panda in your device's external web browser; the dashboard reuses the same Firebase account and security rules as the mobile app.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third party. The only sub-processors involved are:

  • Google LLC — provider of Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Google Play Services used by the app. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Your employer — managers and administrators within your own organisation can see attendance records of the employees they manage, as part of the ordinary purpose of this application.

Data Retention

Attendance punches and profile records are retained for as long as the app is in use by your organisation, so you and your manager can review historical attendance. Edit-request documents are retained in the same way. Local preferences (theme, biometric lock) and the forgot-to-punch-out reminder are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

If your organisation stops using KS Panda, your administrator can request bulk deletion of the organisation's records by contacting us at the email address below.

Data Security

All network traffic between the app and Firebase is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS. Firestore security rules restrict reads and writes to the authenticated owner (and authorised managers/admins). Biometric lock, if enabled, requires an on-device biometric match before the app can be viewed.

Children's Privacy

KS Panda is an employee tool and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us so we can remove the account.

Your Rights

  • You may sign out of the app at any time from the Profile screen.
  • You may clear all local app data (theme preference, biometric setting, reminders) at any time via your device's Settings → Apps → KS Panda → Clear Data.
  • You may request a copy of, or deletion of, your attendance records by contacting us at the email below. Where legally required, we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
  • You may uninstall the app at any time without penalty.
