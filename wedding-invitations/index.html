Common questions

Before you ask us, read these.

Do my guests need to install an app?

No. The invitation opens in any browser — Chrome, Safari, WhatsApp's built-in browser, anything. Just tap the link.

Will it work on older phones?

Yes. Any phone made in the last 5 years handles it beautifully. On older phones, the heavier animations gracefully simplify so nothing breaks.

What if guests open it after the wedding?

The invitation automatically transforms into a thank-you message the day after your wedding, and stays live for 7 more days so late-opening guests still see a warm goodbye.

Can I update the invitation after launch?

Yes. Small changes (like a venue update) are free within the included revision rounds. Larger content changes cost ₹500 each.

How long does the link stay live?

Until 7 days after your wedding. That covers your pre-wedding sharing phase and gives late-opening guests time to see the auto thank-you message. After that, the link gracefully retires — no renewals needed.

Is this only for Odia weddings?

Not at all. The Jagannath invocation and Odia copy are swappable — we build Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, and English-only variants too.

Will my guests' data be collected?

No. The invitation does not track individual guests. No signup, no accounts, no data harvesting. Only anonymous page-view analytics for our internal records.
