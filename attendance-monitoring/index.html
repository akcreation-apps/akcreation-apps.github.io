The problem with traditional attendance

Why Excel sheets and biometric machines fail site-based teams.

Construction crews work at multiple sites. Security staff rotate shifts. Delivery riders move all day. Field sales teams never see the office. Traditional attendance was built for a factory floor — not for teams on the move.

Common questions

Everything you'd want to ask before booking a call.

What makes this different from a biometric or Excel sheet?

Biometric devices tie attendance to one physical location. Excel sheets are easy to fake and hard to verify in real time. Our system uses GPS geofencing — employees can only punch in when physically at a registered site — and the admin sees punch activity live from any device. Multi-site teams, field teams, and construction sites finally get accurate daily attendance without manual verification.

Do my employees need to install an app?

Yes. Employees use a lightweight Android app to punch in and out. The app checks their GPS location against the site boundary before allowing a punch — no spoofed attendance, no clock-in from home. Admins and managers use the web portal in any browser.

How do the three roles work?

Admin sees all sites, employees, and reports. Manager sees only their assigned sites and the employees punched to those sites — they can edit daily punches for their sites. Employee punches in and out via the Android app. Each role sees exactly what they need, nothing more.

Can the system handle multiple work sites?

Yes. You can register unlimited work sites with their GPS coordinates and radius. Each employee is assigned to one or more sites. Managers can be scoped to specific sites too — a Cuttack manager will only see Cuttack-site data, a Bhubaneswar manager only sees their sites.

What if an employee needs a punch to be corrected?

The employee raises an edit request from the app. The admin or assigned manager reviews it, calls the employee to verify if needed, and approves or rejects. Every action is logged in an audit trail so there is never a dispute about who changed what.

How secure is the attendance data?

The system runs on Google Firebase (same infrastructure used by Google's own apps). All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. A daily rotating access code is required on each new device signup to prevent unauthorized employees from joining. Role-based security rules prevent managers from seeing other managers' data.

Can the system be customized for my business?

Yes — this is a fully personalized system, not a generic SaaS app. We build and deploy a dedicated instance for your business: your logo, your brand colors, your shift rules, your custom fields, and specific workflows for your industry. Setup takes 2–4 weeks depending on complexity.

Is there a live demo I can try?

Because each deployment is personalized, there is no public demo. We offer a free 30-minute demo call where we screen-share a reference deployment, walk through the admin, manager, and employee app, and discuss how it would adapt to your business.
