4. Two-Year Commitment & Pro-Rata Clawback on Early Leaving

4.1 The Driver acknowledges that the above premium of ₹14,413/- has been paid by the Vehicle Owner for the benefit associated with this two-year commitment.

4.2 If the Driver voluntarily leaves the service arrangement before 01 October 2028, the Driver shall pay the Vehicle Owner a pro-rata share of the premium calculated as:

Amount Payable = ₹14,413 × (Complete Months Remaining ÷ 24), rounded to the nearest rupee.

4.3 Illustration: If the Driver leaves after serving 18 months, months remaining = 6; amount payable = ₹14,413 × 6 ÷ 24 = ₹3,603/-.

4.4 When the pro-rata amount applies and when it does not:

  • Applies: Driver leaves on his own, or Owner removes him for a reason listed in §8 (drinking on duty, theft, licence cancelled, etc.).
  • Does not apply: Owner himself asks the Driver to leave without any fault, or the Driver stops working due to serious illness, accident, or a genuine family emergency.

5. Driver's Responsibilities

  1. The Driver shall operate the vehicle carefully, responsibly and in accordance with applicable traffic and transport laws.
  2. The Driver shall take reasonable care of the vehicle and promptly inform the Vehicle Owner of any accident, damage, mechanical issue, challan or other material incident.
  3. The Driver shall not intentionally misuse the vehicle or use it for any unlawful purpose.
  4. The Driver shall comply with mutually agreed instructions regarding the vehicle and driving duties.
  5. The Driver shall maintain a valid driving licence and all legally required documents/permissions applicable to the driving duties.

6. Owner's Responsibilities

  1. The Vehicle Owner shall provide the vehicle and communicate the driving/service requirements to the Driver.
  2. The Vehicle Owner shall be responsible for matters relating to the vehicle that are legally or contractually the Owner's responsibility (RC, insurance, PUC, fitness, permits).
  3. The Owner shall communicate any material changes to the Driver in a reasonable manner.

7. Notice Period for Voluntary Resignation

The Driver shall give the Vehicle Owner at least 30 (thirty) days' prior written notice before voluntary resignation. Failure to serve the full notice period shall be treated as the trigger event under §4 and the pro-rata clawback shall become payable in addition to any unpaid dues.

8. Grounds for Termination by the Owner

The Vehicle Owner shall have the right to terminate this Agreement with immediate effect, without compensation to the Driver, in the event of:

  1. Intoxication or drug use while on duty.
  2. Suspension, cancellation or endorsement of the Driver's licence.
  3. Proven theft, dishonesty, or registration of a criminal case against the Driver.
  4. Wilful or grossly negligent damage to the vehicle.
  5. Repeated absence from duty without approved leave.
  6. Unauthorised use of the vehicle for personal or commercial purposes.

Termination on any of the above grounds shall not entitle the Driver to any refund, and the pro-rata clawback under §4 shall apply in full as if the Driver had voluntarily left on the date of termination.

9. Traffic Challans & Fines

All fines, challans or penalties arising from the Driver's personal conduct (over-speeding, signal jump, no helmet/seat-belt, drunk driving, mobile phone use while driving, wrong-side driving, etc.) shall be borne solely by the Driver. Fines arising from lapses in vehicle documents (PUC, fitness certificate, insurance, permit) shall be borne by the Vehicle Owner.

10. Driving Licence Warranty & Document Submission

The Driver warrants that the driving licence submitted is genuine, currently valid, and not under suspension, and undertakes to inform the Vehicle Owner within 24 hours of any suspension, cancellation or endorsement thereof. 11. Return of Vehicle on Separation

On the last working day, the Driver shall return to the Vehicle Owner the vehicle, all keys, RC book, insurance papers, FASTag, fuel card (if any), toolkit, and uniform (if issued), in the condition received, normal wear and tear excepted. A joint handover checklist shall be signed by both parties on the day of handover; any missing or damaged item shall be reasonably valued and adjusted against dues.

12. Confidentiality, Force Majeure & Dispute Resolution

12.1 Confidentiality. The Driver shall not disclose to any third party the Vehicle Owner's or his family's routines, routes, residence details, visitors, or any business or contact information acquired during the course of service. This obligation shall survive termination of this Agreement.

12.2 Force Majeure. Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations hereunder due to events beyond reasonable control, such as natural disaster, pandemic, government-imposed lockdown, civil unrest or war. Obligations shall resume once the event ceases.

12.3 Dispute Resolution. The parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute amicably. Failing that, the dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator mutually appointed under the Arbitration & Conciliation Act, 1996. Seat and venue of arbitration: Cuttack, Odisha; language: English/Odia. Subject to the above, the courts at Cuttack, Odisha shall have exclusive jurisdiction. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

13. Mutual Understanding & Declaration

Both parties confirm that they have read this Agreement, understood its contents and entered into it voluntarily. Both parties agree to maintain a clear record of any payment made under this Agreement. Any amendment to this Agreement shall be made only in writing and signed by both parties.
