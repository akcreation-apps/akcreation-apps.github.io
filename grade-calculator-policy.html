Privacy Policy

Grade Calculator (BPUT Based)

Effective date: September 25, 2026

This policy explains what data the Grade Calculator (BPUT Based) Android app (package com.akcreation_apps.bputcalculator), published by AK Creation, collects, why, and the choices you have.

1. Data you give us

When you set up your profile and save results, the app stores the following on your device and backs it up to our cloud database (Google Firebase Cloud Firestore) so you can restore it later:

  • Name, BPUT registration number, date of birth, college and branch
  • Your saved results: subjects, credits, grades, SGPA, CGPA, marks and percentage
  • Your theme preference

Your registration number and date of birth are used as the key to restore your backup. We use this data only to provide backup and restore. We do not sell it.

2. Data collected automatically

ServiceDataPurpose
Google AdMobAdvertising ID, device and app interaction data, approximate location derived from IPShowing ads (including rewarded ads for in-app coins) and measuring ad performance

These services are provided by Google and follow Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings.

3. Microphone

The SGPA and CGPA screens offer optional voice input. If you allow microphone access, your speech is converted to text by your device's speech recognition service. The app does not record, store or upload audio.

4. Links to other sites

The app opens external pages such as the official BPUT results website and seminar reports on Google Drive. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the Google services listed above, which process it to provide ads, analytics and cloud storage.

6. Security and retention

All data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Backup data is kept until you ask us to delete it. Analytics and advertising data are kept according to Google's retention settings.

7. Deleting your data

To delete your cloud backup, email akcreation072k19@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my data" and include your BPUT registration number. We will delete your profile and results from our database within 30 days and confirm by email. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device.

8. Children

The app is intended for university students aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
